### Hi, I'm Zach. I design the brand and build the product, from idea to IDE.

I'm a design engineer in Williamsport, Pennsylvania, with nearly three decades of designing for the web. I ran my own studio, then founded and shipped my own products. Today I prototype and ship by directing AI coding agents (mostly Claude Code). I own the decisions, the design system and the review, and the agents do the typing.

**Case studies**

- **[Trail Sherpa](https://zachlyon.com/work/trail-sherpa):** an offline trip planner and trail app for the Pennsylvania Wilds. Brand, design language, web planner, admin dashboard, and iOS and Android apps, shipped in five months. Includes the decision log and a chart of 743 commits (85% co-authored with agents).
- **[EvAAL Tools](https://zachlyon.com/work/evaal):** a WordPress site and PHP app rebuilt as one accessible SvelteKit app. Lighthouse accessibility went from 82 to 100.
- **[Hive Science](https://zachlyon.com/work/hive-science):** citizen-science survey apps and research dashboards with UC Davis, the Desert Research Institute and Lynker, partly funded by NASA. Volunteers logged about 25,000 observations.
- **[Joinify](https://zachlyon.com/work/joinify):** a real-time content platform for destination apps, with Bluetooth-beacon walking tours.

**How I work**

1. **Define:** research, the people and the constraints, written down before anything is drawn.
2. **Design:** paper first, then Illustrator. The brand becomes tokens and components.
3. **Develop:** prototype in code with AI agents, guided by project docs and skills I write so the output stays on-brand and consistent.
4. **Deploy:** tests, CI, real devices and real users. Then I measure whether it worked.

**Tools I reach for:** Svelte and SvelteKit, React, TypeScript, MapLibre and PostGIS, Capacitor, Supabase, Cloudflare, Claude Code, Adobe Illustrator.

Most of my client and product work lives in private repos. The public ones here are small, open pieces of how I work.
